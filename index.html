Welcome to Monument Bowling!

An 11-week season of Wednesday practices and Friday matches, with a shot at the Berkshire County championship and the state high school championship.

Cove Lanes. There are a couple of Wednesday matches and a Monday practice too — check the schedule regularly, since changes happen during the season.

Fridays

Berkshire County League matches. The top 4 teams advance to the Berkshire County championship and the state high school championship.

Cost

$120 per person

The user fee covers all practice and match fees. Checks go to the coach directly, who manages team finances.

What You Need to Know

  • Uniform: a team-issued shirt, plus jeans that you provide.
  • Bowling ball: owning one is recommended, but not required. If you can't afford one or aren't sure how serious you are yet, the lanes have plenty of house balls to use.
  • Parent/guardian consent is required.
  • Playing time: in JV matches everyone bowls equally. In varsity matches the coach chooses who bowls when, and playing time is not guaranteed for anyone on the varsity roster.
  • Fundraising: the team raises money for the league with a 50/50 raffle. A Krispy Kreme fundraiser helps pay for team shirts, and any extra can reduce tournament registration fees or cover future equipment for families.
